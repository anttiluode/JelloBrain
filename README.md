# JelloBrain

### Can meaningless signals write a medium that later remembers order, changes context, and computes?

**Live:** https://anttiluode.github.io/JelloBrain/

This repo takes the next step after [GelatinIsland](https://github.com/anttiluode/GelatinIsland). In that world, activity built geometry and geometry routed later activity. Here the traffic becomes a tiny artificial language.

> **Words are pokes that can become roads. Roads change what later pokes can do.**

Six meaningless primitives — `A B C D E F` — enter a writable 2-D dynamical substrate. A pseudoword is only a temporal sequence such as `ABC` or `ACB`. There are no embeddings, pretrained models, semantic labels, or stored word IDs. Fast excitation can be erased while slow directed material remains.

The first question is therefore deliberately smaller than “can it think?”:

> **After the fast activity is gone, did the history of signals leave a functional, order-sensitive operator behind?**

## What is actually growing?

The gel is a grid with four directed conductivities per local edge. Recent activation leaves a medium-timescale eligibility trace. If neighbouring activity follows it in time, slow material is deposited on the corresponding directed edge.

```text
primitive pulses
      ↓
fast excitation + refractory state
      ↓
local temporal co-activity
      ↓
directed slow material
      ↓
new propagation operator
      ↓
future pulses take different trajectories
```

The slow material is the memory. The geometry is the weight matrix, except it is physically distributed over the world rather than stored as a dense array of learned word weights.

## Why W1 and W2 matter most

A weak version of this demo could “remember” merely because corpus A contained more `A`s than corpus B. W1 removes that escape hatch: both corpora contain exactly the same primitive counts. Only order differs.

Then W2 attacks another escape hatch. It takes every material value in a grown world, preserves the entire multiset, and only scrambles spatial position/direction. The held-out decoder falls from `1.00` to `0.50`. So in this implementation, the information is not just “how much material exists”; it is in **material arrangement as an operator**.

## Why the attached communication paper belongs here

Stephens, Silbert & Hasson, *Speaker–listener neural coupling underlies successful communication* (PNAS, 2010, doi:10.1073/pnas.1008662107) is useful for the next step, not as proof of this model. They found spatial/temporal coupling between speaker and listener activity during successful story communication; coupling strongly diminished when the linguistic communication was broken. Listener activity usually lagged the speaker, while some regions showed anticipatory coupling, and stronger anticipatory coupling tracked better comprehension.

That suggests a much better Phase II than simply attaching two gels together. Give two private gels only a narrow pulse channel and a cooperative task, then ask whether **useful coupling**, lead/lag structure, prediction, and repair emerge. The proposed falsification gates are in [DIALOGUE.md](DIALOGUE.md).

## Claim boundary

If these gates hold, the safe statement is:

> **JelloBrain is a small artificial substrate in which temporally ordered signals write persistent spatial structure, that structure changes later signal processing, and the resulting dynamics can perform a useful nonlinear temporal transformation.**

Not “it understands.” Not “it is a brain.” Not yet “it thinks.”

That last question is why the repo has its name.

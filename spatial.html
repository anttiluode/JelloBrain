What S3 actually changed

innovation = log(observed launcher response) − predicted log(self-generated response | cue)

The predictor is calibrated from the body's own cue-driven responses and never sees the reward target. After a wire swap, inherited geometry is still enormous in the raw signal, but it is mostly cancelled in the innovation signal. A lucky counterfactual launch can therefore be sampled. If it earns reward, the normal local JelloWorld write rule deposits material on that cue→launch trajectory. The new route exceeds the old expectation, becomes a positive residual, and gets selected more often.

This is deliberately narrower than “two spatial gels invented language.” The receiver is trivial here.
